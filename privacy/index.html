Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

We do not collect your data

Vasanth.fun does not collect, store, or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no sign-ups, and no user tracking of any kind.

Analytics

We use Cabin, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to understand aggregated usage — things like how many people visit the site and which games are popular. Cabin does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not collect any personally identifiable information. The data we see is entirely anonymous and aggregated.

No cookies

This site does not use cookies. Your high scores are stored locally on your device using localStorage and never leave it.

No personal data

We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or any other personal information. There is nothing to delete, export, or request because we simply do not have it.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to reach out at hello@vasanthv.net.
